# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-11-03
**Source:** User requests and inspected repository. This is a working draft, not a record of customer research.

## Product Overview
AR FARM JAYA is an operational web application for ARFARM BHINNEKA NUSA JAYA, covering warehouse inventory, purchasing, receiving, distribution, retail, store catalogs, invoices, collection letters, and weekly field reports. It is an authenticated company workspace, with Vercel deployment and PostgreSQL persistence. Pricing and a public SaaS offer are not established.

## Target Audience
Company administrators, warehouse staff, and reporting staff. Tasks: record stock movements, prepare branded invoices without retyping catalog items, and produce reports. Do not invent company-size metrics or customer personas beyond these observed roles.

## Positioning and Differentiation
Store-specific identities, catalogs, configurable invoice colors/sections/logo size, live previews, saved invoice design snapshots, and print/PDF output. Warehouse operations and reporting share the same workspace. The login CTA serves existing authorized users; there is no public registration flow.

## Problems and Switching
The user wants professional, neat, simple invoices with detailed headers and independent customization. Manual spreadsheets are the supplied visual reference, not an evaluated competitor. Migration reliability and consistent historical documents matter. Do not claim measured time savings.

## Customer Language
User phrases: “profesional”, “rapi”, “simpel”, “mudah”, “tagline”, “logo”, “customize”. Use concrete Indonesian labels: toko, invoice, katalog barang, jatuh tempo, ongkos kirim, diskon, pratinjau, simpan, cetak.

## Brand Voice
Professional and direct. Explain what a button or setting changes. Keep errors actionable. Describe capabilities rather than adding adjectives such as “terbaik”.

## Proof and Objections
Evidence comes from source and tests, not customer testimonials. No verified user counts, sales totals, conversion uplift, performance guarantees, certifications, or third-party endorsements. Demo charts and figures must be explicitly marked as illustrations. Electronic invoice hashes are internal integrity fingerprints, not certified signatures. Persistent online sync requires configured hosting and a successful save; do not promise complete offline durability or instant collaboration.

## Goals
Help authorized staff enter the workspace, configure a store, select catalog items, save an invoice, and print it. Preserve existing company records during interface changes.

## Scope
The user requested removal of the aquaculture module. Do not feature it in navigation, marketing, or new account setup. Historical server data and backups require separately verified cleanup; removing UI is not proof of production deletion.

## Research, Ads and Pricing
Not supplied. Do not create campaigns, publish outreach, invent pricing, add trackers, or fabricate social proof.

## Learnings
No measured marketing experiments yet.
